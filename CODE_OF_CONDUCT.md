# Code of Conduct

This project exists to help trans people get their documents in order. The community around it has to be safe for the people it serves, including contributors who are themselves trans or nonbinary. That is the bar everything below serves.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language, including people's stated names and pronouns
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the people this project serves
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Misgendering or deadnaming anyone, deliberately or after correction
- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks on contributors
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (physical or email address, legal name, transition status) without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

A note specific to this project: debates about whether trans people should be able to change their documents are out of scope here, in issues, pull requests, and every other project space. This project takes that as settled.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages) and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer by opening a private security advisory on GitHub or emailing the address listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.
