information vermont residency and current address lawful status in the united states (required for real id edl) vermont residents must also provide the following self‑designated descriptors for their credential: height weight eye color gender applicants are not required under vermont or federal law to present documentation that confirms the information they submit for these entries. note: enhanced driver’s licenses (edl) have additional requirements. more information... identity and date of birth you must provide one of the following to obtain a new credential: valid, unexpired passport or birth certificate. if the name on your passport or birth certificate differs from the name that will appear on your credential, you must provide a linking document (certified birth record, marriage certificate, adoption decree, or divorce decree, including translation if applicable). linking documents cannot be used alone to establish identity without a passport or birth certificate. consular report of birth abroad (crba) issued by the u.s. department of state (form fs‑240, ds‑1350, or fs‑545) valid consular identification document issued by mexico, guatemala, or another government with comparable security standards (as determined by the commissioner) valid, unexpired permanent resident card (form i‑551) certificate of naturalization (form n‑550 or n‑570) certificate of citizenship (form n‑560 or n‑561) real id‑compliant driver’s license or identification card issued under 6 cfr part 37 if the name on your license differs from the name on your primary identity document if the name on your credential will differ from the name on your primary identity document (due to marriage, adoption, court order, etc.), you must provide certified evidence of the name change. the dmv will retain copies of the documentation and maintain a record of both the recorded name and the name on the primary source documents. note: additional documentation may be required at the dmv’s discretion if submitted documents appear questionable or if there is reason to doubt identity. dmv personnel will determine whether documents are authentic, valid, and acceptable. photocopies and faxes are not accepted. foreign licenses reciprocity applicants presenting a foreign driver’s license or foreign identity documents may need to provide additional documentation. these resources explain how foreign license featured article august 18, 2026 irp and ifta new account processing delays irp and ifta new account processing delays the vermont department of motor vehicles commercial vehicle office is advising customers to expect delays in the processing of new international registration plan (irp) and international fuel tax agreement (ifta) accounts . please note that before a new irp or ifta account can be established, vermont is required to verify that the applicant meets applicable residency and/or established place of business requirements . this process requires the submission and review of supporting documentation. customers applying for a new account should be prepared to provide the required documentation and allow additional time for review and processing. applications that are incomplete or require additional documentation may take longer to process or be able to obtain their credentials. to help avoid unnecessary delays: submit all required documentation with your application. ensure documents are current, legible, and clearly identify the applicant and vermont location. respond promptly to any requests from the commercial vehicle office for additional information or documentation. do not assume that an application is complete until all required residency or established place of business requirements have been verified. the commercial vehicle office appreciates your patience and cooperation as we complete these required verification procedures. these measures help ensure vermont remains compliant with irp and ifta requirements and maintains the
