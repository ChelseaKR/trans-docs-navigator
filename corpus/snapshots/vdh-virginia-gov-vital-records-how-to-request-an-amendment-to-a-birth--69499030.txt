an amendment or correction to a birth certificate, we will not know what is necessary until your request is made and we can review the information. an administration fee of $10.00 is charged for any change to a certificate. if you wish to receive a copy of the certificate once amended, the total cost will be $25.00 including the $15.00 certificate fee and the administration fee. please complete the online form, or use a form below, and mail in the information you have. we will get back with you on what is necessary to fulfill your request. for information specific to changing the sex
