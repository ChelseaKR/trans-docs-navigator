regional office and require supporting legal documentation. update your name update your name with the social security administration you must update your name with the social security administration required. replace on mydrive portal request by mail visit an office frequently asked questions open all close all can i update my address online? yes. eligible customers may update their address online through mydrive. a connected account is required, and valid proof of your new kentucky address must be uploaded. can i change my name online or by mail? no. name changes must be completed in person and require supporting legal documentation. can i order a replacement online? yes. eligible customers may request a replacement online through mydrive. a connected account is required. do i need to bring all required real id documents when requesting a replacement real id? no. if all information on your current real id is up to date, no documentation is required. if you need to update your name or address, you will need to provide supporting documents. is a vision screening required to update information on my credential? no. a vision screening is required only if you are within your renewal window and choose to renew at the same time. how long will it take to receive my new credential? once processed, you will receive a temporary document. your permanent credential typically arrives by mail within 10–15 business days. will i receive a temporary credential? yes. customers receive a temporary credential while waiting for the permanent card to arrive by mail. am i required to fill out a pre-application online before visiting a regional office? no. completing a pre-application through mydrive is not required to visit an office, but it can help ensure you bring the
