id search transparency contact dsb proof of domicile documents accepted: all applicants applying for a driver's license or identification card must provide two proofs of mississippi residency. any item presented to establish proof of residency must include the applicant's name and a mississippi residence address (no p.o. boxes will be accepted). no junk mail/soliciting, no envelopes, no handwritten documents/letters will be accepted. utility bill (electric, water, gas, or cable) bank statement with physical address (no blank checks accepted) paycheck stub (if it has name and address) (must be returned taxes) employer id card with address firearm permit pilot’s license school record/transcript/report card vehicle title, vehicle registration or car tag registration receipt voter military orders or military documents any official government (city, county, state or federal) document or letter with applicant’s address. homestead exemption receipt notarized employer verification on their letterhead stating address of applicant (must include phone number) sex offender registration documents applicants under 21 years of age may use documentation that applies to their parent’s or guardian’s domicile. applicants 21 years and older: if no bills or similar documents feature the name of the applicant, the applicant may use the spouse or parent, but must produce a marriage license or birth certificate proving legal status. in the case of roommates, landlords, parents, or guardians, the applicant may use the proof of domicile affidavit in conjunction with a driver’s license or identification card (same address as applicant) or one acceptable document to complete the requirement of two proofs of domicile. any additional documentation or explanation you wish to provide to support your claim for residency maybe presented. acceptance is at the sole discretion of the driver service bureau. acceptable documents to verify date of birth : birth certificate (original), small card or full size will now be accepted as long as they have a state seal and certificate number. certificate must be original or certified copy.
