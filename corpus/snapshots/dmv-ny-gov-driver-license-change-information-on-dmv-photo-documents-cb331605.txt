information about for most changes, you will need to visit your local dmv office and bring proof of the change. unless otherwise noted, the fee for an amended permit or license is $12.50, and the fee for an amended non-driver id card is $5.00. once ordered, your new document will come in 2 weeks. we will give you a temporary non-photo driver license, learner permit, or non-driver id at the dmv. you will receive your new document in the mail in about 2 weeks. for information on how to change your address on dmv documents and records, see change address . if you plan on traveling we recommend you come to the dmv now to get an enhanced or real id and avoid a second office visit later. learn about enhanced, real id, and standard documents . complete this guide to make sure you have to correct proofs of identification you need. also, bring any additional information needed for your specific change listed below. find the documents you need to get a real id or enhanced permit, using your personal information or by logging into your mydmv profile using ny.gov . at a dmv office you can change the gender on your license or id card by completing the application for permit, driver license or non-driver id card (pdf) mv-44 and visiting your local dmv office. find office locations remove corrective lenses restriction you must prove that you can pass a vision test without corrective lenses. see more information about removing a corrective lenses restriction . correct an error (mistake) on your record if we made an error on your document, use the ask dmv a question service to request a correction. we may be able to correct it without you coming to a dmv office. there is no fee for a correction. change photo you must go to a dmv office to change your photo. when you renew there is no additional fee to update your photo when it is not time to renew bring the items listed below to your local dmv office a completed application for permit, driver license or non-driver id card (pdf) (mv-44) your current license, permit, or non-driver id (you must surrender the document) $17.50 to update the photo on a license or permit, and $13 for a non-driver photo id.
