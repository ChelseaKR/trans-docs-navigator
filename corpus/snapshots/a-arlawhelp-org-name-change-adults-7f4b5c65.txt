search words search name change name change introduction name change for adults name change for minors introduction an adult may ask an arkansas circuit court to change their legal name. arkansas law says a circuit court may change a person s name if there is a good reason for the change. common reasons may include using a name that better reflects your identity, using a name you are already known by, religious reasons, family reasons, or personal safety reasons. a judge may deny a name change if the request is being made for fraud, to avoid debts, to hide from law enforcement, or for another improper purpose. a court order is usually required to change your legal name for reasons other than marriage, divorce, or correcting a small mistake on a record. some small mistakes on a birth certificate may be corrected through the arkansas department of health without going to court. each case is different. contact vital records to ask what documents are needed. legal sources ark. code ann. 9-2-101 , name change - procedure. ark. code ann. 9-2-102 . use of new name. arkansas department of health, change or amend a birth or death certificate arkansas department of health, correct minor errors on a birth or death record arlawhelp.org, fee waiver forms step 1: prepare a cover sheet you will need to prepare a cover sheet to accompany the request to change your name. include the following information: county filing date plaintiff (your name) address select no for jury trial requested select (nc) for type of case select original for manner of filing civil cover sheet this is to be filed with your name change. see instructions for how to fill out civil cover sheet-instructions this is helpful when filling out your civil cover sheet step 2: prepare and notarize your petition for name change to ask for a legal name change, file a written petition for name change in the circuit court where you live. you may use the interactive form linked below. the petition should include: your current legal name your date of birth the county where you live the new name you are requesting the reason for the name change your contact information it must be verified, which means it is signed under oath. take the petition for name change to a notary public to have it notarized. do not sign any forms until you a re in front of the notary public. notarial services are often available at bank branches and various government agencies (a small service charge may be required). alternatively, a notary can be found in your area by conducting a search of the secretary of state s database. follow the instructions below to file in the circuit court in the county where you live. interactive form - name change petition for adults this link opens in a new window step 3: file your petition take your original and two copies of your petition for name change to the circuit clerk s office for filing. the circuit clerk will require a filing fee of around $165. an exact amount can be determined by contacting the clerk s office in the county where you live. payment will be accepted in cash or by check/money order made payable to [county name] county circuit court clerk. if you cannot afford to pay the filing fee , then you can file an in forma pauperis (ifp) petition. the petition, supporting affidavit, and order are available on this website at fee waiver information . you must get the in forma pauperis order signed by a judge before you can file the name change without paying the filing fees. if the ifp petition is granted, after filing the form with the clerk the judge will enter an order waiving your filing fee. step 4: present your order to a judge after filing the petition for name change ask the circuit clerk about the procedure for presenting an order to the judge in that county. depending on the county: there may be a set day to present your case in court, or you may have to contact the judge s trial court administrator to be placed on the docket. you may need to submit the proposed order to the judge without a formal hearing when you are in front of the judge you will be sworn in. give the judge a copy of the petition and order. once you are sworn in you must present testimony. the judge may ask you questions or have you present testimony on your own. you should tell her the following information: my current name is __________________ my date of birth is ___________________ my driver s license number or social security number is __________________. i would like to change my name to _________________________ because__________________________________________________ i understand that changing my name does not change any of my rights or responsibilities. that i am not asking for the name change for fraud, to avoid debts, to hide from law enforcement, or for another improper purpose. the judge may ask you additional questions. if the judge approves your petition, she will sign the order and give them back to you. your name change is not finalized until you file that order with the circuit clerk. step 5: file the order and civil disposition sheet if the judge approves the name change, the judge will sign an order. your name change is not final until the signed order is filed with the circuit clerk. the order should include: your old name your date of birth your new name any identifying information the court requires, such as a social security number or driver s license number because identifying information is confidential, the court file should include a redacted version for the public record. redacted means private information is removed or blacked out. the clerk must make sure identifying information is not publicly available, except for your old name and new name. the clerk must send the unredacted order to: arkansas crime information center arkansas driver control division of vital records, if requested if you ask the clerk to send the order to vital records, you will still need to pay the arkansas department of health s fees to amend your birth certificate and get a new certified copy. you may need certified copies of the signed order to update your name with other agencies, such as: arkansas department of health/vital records social security administration driver services passport office banks and financial institutions schools employers medical and insurance records note that even if you request that the clerk send a copy to the division of vital records, you will still need to pay the department of vital records fees, for them to update your birth certificate and provide you with a new copy. visit their website for more details . important note a name change does not erase debts, criminal history, child support obligations, immigration records, or other legal responsibilities. after your name is changed by court order, arkansas law says you are known by the new name and may sue or be sued under that new name. previous: name change
