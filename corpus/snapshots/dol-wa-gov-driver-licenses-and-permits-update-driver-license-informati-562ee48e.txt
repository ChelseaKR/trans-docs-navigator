to change the gender designation on your driver license. we offer 3 options: m (male), f (female) and x (not exclusively male or female). change your standard driver license, id card, or permit you can change your gender designation on your standard driver license, id card, or permit either in person or by mail. an online option is not available. request a change in person to request a change in person, make an appointment to visit a driver licensing office . at the appointment, you’ll need to: bring your current license or id card with you. verbally confirm your gender designation. request a change by mail to request a change by mail, you’ll need to complete a change of gender designation request form and mail it to the address on the form. after we process the form, we’ll mail you a letter to get a new card. to get a new card, you can: log into license express and get your new card online. make an appointment to visit a driver licensing office . if you visit an office, don’t forget to bring your current license, id card, or permit with you. change your enhanced driver license or enhanced id (edl/eid) you can change your gender designation on your enhanced driver license or enhanced id (edl/eid) either in person or by mail. an online option is not available. request a change in person to request a change in person, make an appointment to visit a driver licensing office . at the appointment, you’ll need to: bring your current license or id card with you. bring a completed change of gender designation request form . request a change by mail to request a change by mail, you’ll need to complete a change of gender designation request form and mail it to the address on the form. after we process the form, we’ll mail you a letter to get a new card. to get a new card, you can: log into license express and get your new card online. make an appointment to visit a driver licensing office . if you visit an office, don’t forget to bring your current license, id card, or permit with you. additional fees may apply if you're only updating your gender designation, your new card is free. if you need to update more than your gender, there may be an additional fee for: adding other endorsements at the same time. updating other information on your card like your name or address. renewing your license – you can renew up to a year in advance. print was this
