questions open all close all what documents are required for an id card? to apply for a kentucky id card, you will need: birth certificate social security card proof of residency name change document, if your current full legal name, date of birth, or gender is different than what is displayed on your identity or lawful status document. list of approved documents pdf can i have both a kentucky driver's license or permit and an id card? no. effective july 15, 2024, a person may not be issued a personal identification card if they currently hold a valid kentucky instruction permit or operator’s license. a person may not hold more than one license or personal identification card at the same time. can i renew my id card online? yes, if eligible. customers may renew an id card online through mydrive. a verified mydrive account is required. mydrive portal can i upgrade to a real id online or by mail? no. real id upgrades cannot be completed online or by mail. you must visit a regional office and bring the required documents . can someone under 18 get an id card? yes. if the applicant is under 18, a parent or guardian must be present. a parent or guardian may also provide proof of residency in their own name to show the applicant lives with them. how can someone without a permanent address apply for an id card? if the applicant does not have a fixed or permanent address, they may provide a signed letter from a homeless shelter, health care facility, or social service agency stating they are currently receiving services and are a kentucky resident. this letter may be used as proof of residency for a homeless id card. what is the cost of an id card? a standard identification card costs $11.50 for four years or $23.00 for eight years. a child id card costs $6.00 for four years. homeless id cards and voter id cards may be issued at no cost for customers who meet eligibility requirements. what documents do i need to renew or replace my id card? if the information listed on your current id card is up to date, you do not need to bring any documents to renew or request a replacement. additional documents are only required if you are: updating your name changing your address upgrading to a real id if you are renewing or replacing in person, bring any documents that apply to your specific situation to help avoid delays. view valid document types
