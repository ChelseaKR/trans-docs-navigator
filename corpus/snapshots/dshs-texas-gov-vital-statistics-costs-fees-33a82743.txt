and non-transferable, even if the record is not found or is identified incorrectly. note: for in-person orders at dshs in austin, payment made by credit card requires a $2.25 processing fee. the expedited service fee, which shortens processing time, has changed from $5 to $25 to cover the costs for the service. please note the expedited service fee is to expedite our application processing, and does not guarantee that the requested record will be provided if all applications requirements are not met. for expedited service, the application must be mailed to us via an overnight mail
