corrections name changes/birth certificate corrections you must file for a name change in the county in which you presently live and are a resident, regardless of where you were born. individuals 18 years of age and over may change their names without parental consent. you must have a birth certificate and proof of identity and residency. examples of proof of identity: • passport • driver’s license • state, school, or work id examples of proof of residency: • utility bill • check stub • city or county tax bill i n the case of a minor change of name , if both parents are listed on the birth certificate, they both must be present with proper identification. a notarized affidavit from the absent parent will be accepted and that form is available in our office or on our website (under name change/birth certificate correction forms). court costs: cash or credit card • in-person name change -$166.50 birth certificate correction- $216.50 • virtual/zoom ( includes $3.50 order mailing fee ) name change- $170.00 birth certificate correction- $220.00 filing hours: • name change petitions can be filed in the probate court clerk's office monday through friday, 9am-3pm. • after filing your petition, you will be scheduled a hearing date for the upcoming thursday afternoon between 1:00 and 2:00. this appointment can be in person or virtually through zoom. • after the court hearing, a copy of any order signed by the judge will be distributed to the petitioner immediately following in-person hearings or mailed to the petitioner at the address left with the clerk's office at the time
