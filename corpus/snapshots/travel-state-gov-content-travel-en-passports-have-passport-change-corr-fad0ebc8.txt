correct a passport learn how to correct or update your u.s. passport due to a name change, data error, or printing error. change your name the process to change your name on your passport varies depending on your situation. expand all it’s been less than 1 year since both your passport was issued and your name was legally changed submit the following by mail: form ds-5504 (find mailing instructions on the form) your most recent u.s. passport an original or certified document showing your name change within 1 year since we issued your most recent u.s. passport. examples include a marriage certificate, divorce decree, or court order. 1 passport photo you will not have to submit any fees for this service. if you want expedited processing, you will have to pay a $60 fee. it’s been more than 1 year since either your passport was issued or your name was legally changed you may be eligible to renew your passport by mail. if you are not eligible to renew by mail, you can apply in person. to renew by mail, you must provide a certified copy of your legal name change document. you can apply in person if you have a valid id in your new name and you complete the marriage details section on page 2 of the form ds-11. renew by mail find out if you're eligible to renew your passport and learn what to submit with your application. apply in person learn what you need to submit for a new passport if you are not eligible for renewal. you are already using a different name but do not have a legal document to show the change if you cannot show your name change with a court order, divorce decree, or marriage certificate, apply in person and submit: form ds-11 your evidence of u.s. citizenship valid id issued in the name you are currently using and photocopy of that id 1 color passport photo fees , which are different depending on your age you need to fill out form ds-60: affidavit regarding a change of name if your name is different than what is on your citizenship evidence and you cannot show your new name through marriage or on a court order. this form must be completed by 2 people who have known you by both names. you must also submit 3 certified or original public records that show you have used the new name for 5 years or more. correct a data or printing error you can correct your passport if there is a data or printing error. a data error may include an issue with your name, sex, or place of birth. a printing error may include discoloration, crooked printing, or missing data on the biographical page. we will correct the error at no charge if the passport is still valid. to correct a data or printing error, submit the following by mail: form ds-5504 (find mailing instructions on the form) your current passport 1 color passport photo evidence of the error, like your u.s. birth certificate showing the correct spelling of your last name you will not have to submit any fees. if you are reporting the error within 1 year of us issuing your incorrect passport, the new passport will be valid for 10 years (adults) and 5 years (children under age 16). if you are reporting the error after 1 year, the new passport will be valid until the expiration date of your original, incorrect passport. change your address if you already have a passport and have moved to a new address, you do not need to update your current passport with your new address. if you have an application in process and have moved to a new address, dial 877-487-2778 or 888-874-7793 (tdd/tty) to update the mailing address on your passport application. change your passport photo if your photo has a printing error, you can use form ds-5504 to reapply at no cost to you. if your photo is printed correctly and you need to update your photo, you may use form ds-82 (if eligible) to reapply. you must pay the normal fees. related information passport photos learn u.s. passport photo requirements. get tips on size, quality, lighting, expression, and acceptable attire. passport fees find how much a u.s. passport costs for adults and children. learn fees, payment methods, and prices for renewals, expedited service, and extra options. passport forms find the
