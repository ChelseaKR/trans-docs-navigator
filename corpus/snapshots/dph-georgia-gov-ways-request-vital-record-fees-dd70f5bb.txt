georgia law requires pre-payment before a record or a service can be provided. fees are non- refundable after a service has been provided. records are sent first class mail, unless expedited shipping is requested (online only). if the record is found, a certified certificate is included in the cost. if the record is not found, a not-on-file letter will be issued. fee structure fee structure birth certificates - $25.00 + $5.00 for additional copies death certificates - $25.00 + $5.00 for additional copies marriage certificates - $10.00 + $5.00 for additional copies fetal death certificates - $10.00 + $5.00 for additional copies paternity acknowledgements - certified copy $10.00 + $10.00 for additional copies current year corrections - free amendments - $10.00 + cost of the certificate acceptable forms of payment *we do not accept personal checks. credit/debit cards (walk-in service or online orders) cash (walk-in service only) certified check money order page last updated 3/11/2026
